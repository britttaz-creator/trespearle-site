For the Love of Dogs

Because your best friend deserves a little indulgence.

Chic, playful pearl stretch necklaces thoughtfully made for pups with impeccable taste.

Jewelry, but make it dog.

Très Perlé creates joyful, elevated adornments for the dogs who make everyday life more beautiful. We started with a simple idea: our dogs are part of our world, our outfits, our photos, and our celebrations — so why should their accessories look like an afterthought?

Ordinary dog accessories are built for function alone. Très Perlé is built differently. Every piece is designed with the same care you'd give a fine piece of your own jewelry, drawing on the same pearls, colors, and details you'd find in a boutique jewelry case — sized down, and stretched just right, for a very good dog.

"A little stretch. A perfect fit."

Each necklace is hand-strung on flexible elastic and designed to slip gently over your dog's head, no clasps required. Whether it's Classicé's timeless ivory pearls, Rosé's blush warmth, or Noiré's midnight contrast, every style is made to be noticed — at the park, in photos, or simply curled up at home.

Très Perlé is a small, dog-loving brand, and every order is made with that same personal care — from the way it's strung to the way it's packaged and shipped to your door.
