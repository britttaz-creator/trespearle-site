Details

A dainty stretch necklace strung with faux pearls, designed to slip gently over your pet's head — no clasps, no hardware to wrestle with. Choose from our signature pearl styles in five sizes, with an optional birthstone or custom charm.

Materials + Care

Faux pearls hand-strung on flexible elastic; charms are gold-tone with crystal accents. Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth and remove before bath time or swimming.

Safety

Not designed for leash attachment or restraint. Remove during rough play, crating, or when your pet is unattended.

Shipping + Returns

Made to order — please allow 10–14 business days. Complimentary shipping on orders $100+, otherwise a flat $9.95. All sales are final. Questions? Send us a message.
