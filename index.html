Details

A dainty stretch necklace strung with faux pearls, designed to slip gently over your dog's head — no clasps, no hardware to wrestle with. Available in three signature styles and five sizes, with an optional charm or accent pearl.

Fit + Stretch

Each necklace is designed with comfortable stretch for an easy on-and-off fit. Sized by neck measurement — see the size guide for the full chart.

Materials

Faux pearls hand-strung on flexible elastic. Charm and accent pearl add-ons are gold-tone with faceted crystal accents.

Care

Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth. Store away from moisture and remove before bath time or swimming.

Safety

Decorative pet jewelry — not designed for leash attachment or restraint. Remove during rough play, crating, or when your pet is unattended.

Shipping + Returns

Made to order — please allow 10–14 business days. Complimentary shipping on orders $75+. All sales are final; we're unable to offer returns or refunds. Questions about your order? Email us.
