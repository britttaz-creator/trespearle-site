Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a size?

Measure around your pup's neck where you'd like the necklace to rest, then match it to our chart: XS (7–9"), S (9–11"), M (11–14"), L (14–18"), XL (18–22"). See the full size guide. Between sizes? Choose the larger size.

Do Très Perlé necklaces stretch?

Yes — each necklace is designed with comfortable stretch for an easy on-and-off fit.

How do I put it on my dog?

Gently stretch the necklace and slip it over your dog's head — there are no clasps or hardware to fuss with.

Can I attach a leash to it?

No. Très Perlé necklaces are decorative pet jewelry designed for style, not restraint, and are not intended for leash attachment.

Can my dog wear it all day?

Supervised wear is recommended. We suggest removing it during rough play, crating, or whenever your pet is unattended.

How should I care for it?

Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth and store away from moisture. Remove before bath time or swimming.

What if my dog's head is larger than their neck?

Measure around the widest part of their head as well, to make sure the necklace can comfortably slip over it.

How quickly will my order ship?

Each necklace is made to order — please allow 10–14 business days to be made and shipped. Orders $75+ ship complimentary.

Do you accept returns or refunds?

Because each necklace is made to order, all sales are final and we're unable to offer returns or refunds. See our Shipping & Returns page.

Can I add a charm or accent pearl?

Yes — add a charm or accent pearl for +$8 at checkout and tell us your preferred style in the notes field. We'll confirm the exact charm with you before your order ships.

Still have a question?
