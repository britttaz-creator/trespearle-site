Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Every Très Perlé necklace is made to order. Please allow 10–14 business days from the time your order is placed for it to be made and shipped.

Complimentary shipping on all orders $75 and over.

Returns & Refunds

Because each necklace is made to order, all sales are final. We're unable to offer returns, exchanges, or refunds.

Please double-check your color, size, and any charm notes at checkout — our size guide can help you choose with confidence.

Questions About Your Order?

Reach out to Brette any time at brettekey@hotmail.com — we're happy to help.
